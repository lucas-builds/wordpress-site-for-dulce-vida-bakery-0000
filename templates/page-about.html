Our Story

A family bakery since 1998

Dulce Vida Bakery began in 1998, when our grandmother opened a small kitchen in Little Havana with little more than her recipes and a love of baking.

What started as a handful of pastelitos sold to neighbors grew into a bakery the whole community came to know. Her recipes for guayaba pastelitos and croquetas haven't changed — and neither has the care that goes into every batch.

Today, our family still runs the ovens every morning, mixing the same dough by hand and baking each custom cake to order, just the way she taught us.
