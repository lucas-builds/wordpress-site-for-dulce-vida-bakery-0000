Our Family Story

Started in 1998, with love

Dulce Vida Bakery was started by our grandmother in 1998, right here in Little Havana. What began as a small kitchen filled with the smell of guava and fresh-baked bread has grown into a neighborhood staple — but it's still run by the same family, with the same recipes.

Every pastelito, croqueta, and cake we make carries that same care and tradition. We're proud to be part of the Little Havana community, and we love seeing familiar faces come through our doors every week.

Still a family affair

From the ovens to the counter, Dulce Vida is run by our family, for our community. Stop by and taste the tradition for yourself.
