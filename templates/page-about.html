Our Family Story

Started by our grandmother in 1998

Dulce Vida began in 1998, when our grandmother opened a small bakery in Little Havana to share the recipes she had carried with her — guava pastelitos, savory croquetas, and the kind of cakes made for celebrating together.

What started as a tiny counter has grown into a neighborhood gathering spot, but the way we bake hasn't changed. Everything is still made by hand, in small batches, the same way she taught us.

A family affair, still today

Three generations of our family have worked behind this counter. We mix the dough by hand, fill every pastelito ourselves, and build every cake to order — because that's what makes it taste like home.

Stop by and say hello, or give us a call if you're planning something special.
